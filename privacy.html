Privacy Policy — Adigo Taxi

Last updated: February 11, 2026

Adigo Taxi (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the Adigo Taxi mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our Taxi and Parcel (Post) services.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information:

  • Phone Number: Required for SMS and Telegram authentication.
  • User ID: Firebase unique identifier to manage your account.
  • Telegram ID: If you choose Telegram login, we store your ID for authentication only.

Service Data:

  • Parcel Details: Weight, type of item (documents, clothes, etc.), and the receiver’s phone number for parcel delivery services.
  • Order History: Details of your previous taxi rides and parcel deliveries.
  • Suggested Price: If you use the "Suggest Price" feature, we store your bid.

Location Information:

  • We collect pickup and destination addresses.
  • Approximate location data may be used via Google Maps/Firebase to help you select addresses accurately.

Technical Information:

  • Device type, operating system, app version, and FCM (Firebase Cloud Messaging) tokens for notifications.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and manage taxi and parcel delivery services.
  • To authenticate users and prevent fraudulent accounts.
  • To send push notifications regarding order status (e.g., "Driver Found").
  • To facilitate communication between passengers, drivers, and parcel receivers.
  • To calculate smart pricing and discounts based on passenger count.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Your information is shared only with:

  • Drivers: Your phone number and pickup/drop-off locations are shared with the driver assigned to your order.
  • Receivers: For parcel services, the receiver may see delivery updates.
  • Service Providers: Google Firebase (for database, authentication, and notifications).
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Data Security

All data is transmitted securely via SSL/TLS and stored using Firebase Security Standards. We implement strict database rules to ensure that only you can access your personal information and order history.

5. Account and Data Deletion

You have the right to delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, your phone number and order history will be permanently removed from our active database.

  • How to delete: Go to the "Profile" section in the app or email us at adigotaxi@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account".

6. Children’s Privacy

Adigo Taxi is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
