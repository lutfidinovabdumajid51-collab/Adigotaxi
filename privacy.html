Privacy Policy – ADIGO TAXI

Last Updated: March 10, 2026

ADIGO TAXI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use the ADIGO TAXI mobile application.

1. Authentication and Login

Users can create an account and sign in to the app using Google Sign-In.

  • We access only basic information from your Google account such as your name, email address, and profile photo.
  • We never access or store your Google password.

2. Information We Collect

To provide ride services and ensure safety, the application may collect the following data:

  • Contact Information: After signing in, users may be asked to provide a phone number so passengers and drivers can communicate.
  • Location Data (GPS): The app collects precise location data to match passengers with nearby drivers and determine pickup locations.
  • Driver Information: If you register as a driver, we may collect vehicle information, driver experience, age, and a photo of your driver's license for verification.
  • Media Files: Users may upload profile photos, vehicle images, and payment receipts.
  • Usage Information: We may collect technical information such as device type, app version, and crash logs to improve the service.

3. How We Use the Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Connect passengers with drivers.
  • Verify driver identity for safety purposes.
  • Enable chat communication between users.
  • Improve service quality and fix technical issues.
  • Ensure safe and reliable transportation services.

4. Sharing Information with Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

However, the application may use secure third-party services to operate the platform:

  • Google Firebase – authentication, database (Firestore), and file storage.
  • Google Cloud – analytics services or AI tools such as Gemini API (if enabled).

5. Data Security

We take appropriate security measures to protect your data. All data is securely stored on Google Firebase servers using industry-standard security systems. Sensitive documents such as driver licenses are accessible only to authorized administrators.

6. Account and Data Deletion

Users have the right to request deletion of their account and personal data at any time.

You can request deletion by:

  • Sending a request through the Profile section of the app.
  • Emailing us with the subject "Account Deletion" at: adigotaxi@gmail.com

All requested data will be permanently deleted from our system within 24 hours.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
