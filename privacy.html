Privacy Policy for Adigo Taxi

Last updated: January 26, 2026

Adigo Taxi ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Our application uses Firebase services to operate securely and efficiently. We collect only the minimum data required to provide our services.

  • Anonymous User Identifier (UID): We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to create a unique, non-personal identifier for each user session.
  • Device Information (Device ID): We may collect a unique device identifier to prevent abuse, improve security, and associate orders with a specific device.
  • Order Information: Pickup location, drop-off location, phone number (if provided), price offers, and notes entered by the user.
  • Push Notification Token: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token used to send order updates and important notifications.
  • App Usage Data: Basic diagnostics such as crash logs and performance data.

❗ We do NOT require user registration. We do NOT collect names, emails, passwords, or social media accounts.

2. Device ID Usage

The Device ID is used strictly for:

  • Fraud prevention
  • Order ownership verification
  • Security and abuse detection

Device ID is NOT used for advertising, tracking across apps, or sold to third parties.

3. How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used to:

  • Process taxi and delivery orders
  • Connect users with drivers
  • Send order status notifications
  • Maintain application security
  • Improve app stability and performance

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is securely stored on Google Firebase servers. We use Firebase Security Rules to ensure that users can access only their own data.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with unauthorized parties.

5. Third-Party Services

Adigo Taxi uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

These services operate under their own privacy policies provided by Google.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may delete their orders directly within the app. Since we use anonymous authentication, all data is linked to the device session.

If you request complete data removal, you may contact us using the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
