Account Deletion – ADIGO TAXI

Last Updated: March 10, 2026

If you want to delete your ADIGO TAXI account and all associated data, you can request account deletion by contacting us via email.

How to Request Account Deletion

  • Send an email to: adigotaxi@gmail.com
  • Use the subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Include the email address used in the ADIGO TAXI app

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • User profile information
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Driver verification documents
  • Uploaded photos
  • Ride and chat history

Processing Time

All deletion requests are processed within 24 hours.

Contact

  • Email: adigotaxi@gmail.com
  • Telegram: @adigo_admin
